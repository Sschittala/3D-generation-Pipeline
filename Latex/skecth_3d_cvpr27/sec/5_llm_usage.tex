\section*{LLM Usage Statement}
OpenAI Codex was used extensively for manuscript drafting, restructuring,
and editing; literature identification, summarization, and bibliography
preparation; suggestions for input-adaptation and ablation designs; and
implementation and debugging of preprocessing, experiment, evaluation, and
analysis scripts. It also assisted with summarizing archived measurements,
preparing tables and figures, and LaTeX formatting. The authors retain
responsibility for verifying the references, code, measurements, and scientific
claims. Implemented experimental procedures are distinguished from completed
experiments, and unexecuted comparisons are not presented as measured results.
